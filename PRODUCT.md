# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Brain Dump is for Mac users who collect fragments while working: copied notes, URLs, screenshots, UI references, product ideas, repo links, and loose observations. They are usually mid-flow and need capture to be instant, quiet, and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

Brain Dump is a native Mac memory layer. It saves raw fragments immediately, processes them in the background, and later surfaces evidence-backed themes when enough signal accumulates. Success means the user can dump something in seconds, trust it was preserved, and review what the system understood without feeling forced into folders, tags, chat, or project planning.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, native. The app should feel closer to Codex Desktop, Raycast, and Linear than to a dashboard, generic notes app, or AI builder. It should look composed and useful at small sizes, with density where it helps and restraint everywhere else.

## Anti-references

Do not make Brain Dump feel like a browser extension, chatbot-first note app, analytics dashboard, generic dark admin shell, or project generator. Avoid bulky panels, decorative cards, washed-out dark grays, debug-like metadata, and empty expanses that make the app feel unfinished.

## Design Principles

1. Capture first, interpret later.
2. Evidence and interpretation stay visually distinct.
3. Native Mac affordances should do the heavy lifting.
4. The interface should feel calm and dense, not sparse and hollow.
5. Any surfaced intelligence must explain why it exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Use system fonts, semantic colors, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus behavior, and reduced-motion-friendly state changes. Avoid relying on color alone for source type, status, or processing state.
